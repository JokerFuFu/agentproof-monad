# Quiet Evidence

Form communicates through a compact sheet, a stable frame and one decisive diagonal. The geometry should feel measured and deliberately constructed. Every curve and margin receives the same care; the mark must remain legible at a glance.

Deep blue supplies the field, warm paper defines the object, and a single clear accent identifies the recorded decision. The restrained palette keeps the work calm. Color carries hierarchy without decorative effects.

Negative space separates the artifact from its surroundings. Internal lines create a short rhythm, while a small seal supplies an asymmetric counterweight. Carefully balanced proportions make the composition useful at both icon and presentation scale.

No explanatory text belongs inside the mark. Its craft lives in crisp edges, consistent spacing and a clean silhouette. The subtle reference is an unchanged document with an attached decision: a receipt, rather than a promise that the contents are correct.
